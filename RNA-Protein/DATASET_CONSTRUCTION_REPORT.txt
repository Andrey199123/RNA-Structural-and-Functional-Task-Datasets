DATASET SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Final dataset size: 246,458 sequences
Length range: 50-2000 nucleotides
Source: RNAcentral database (40.7M sequences)
Location: data/rnacentral_subset_simple/final_subset.fasta

CONSTRUCTION METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Length Filtering
- Filtered RNAcentral sequences to length range 50-2000 nt
- Result: 38,191,793 sequences

Step 2: Quality Filtering  
- Removed sequences with >5% N's (ambiguous nucleotides)
- Result: 38,191,793 sequences (minimal filtering needed)

Step 3: Random Sampling
- Randomly sampled 400,000 sequences from 38.2M
- Used random seed (42) for reproducibility
- Ensures representative coverage across RNA types and lengths
- Time: ~1.5 minutes

Step 4: Deduplication (CD-HIT-EST)
- Identity threshold: 95%
- Removes near-duplicate sequences to avoid redundancy
- Alignment coverage: 80% for both sequences
- Threads: 12 (parallel processing)
- Result: 246,458 unique sequences (38.4% redundancy removed)
- Time: ~15 minutes

RATIONALE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Why sample FIRST, then deduplicate?
- Deduplicating 38M sequences would take weeks
- Random sampling from 38M gives representative diversity
- Deduplication on sample removes redundancy efficiently
- Final dataset is both diverse AND non-redundant

Why 95% identity threshold?
- Removes near-duplicates and sequencing variants
- Faster than 90% threshold (hours vs days)
- Still provides sufficient diversity for ML training
- Balances redundancy removal with computational feasibility

Why this approach meets your requirements?
- Representative: Random sampling preserves distribution of RNA types
- Diverse: 246k sequences from broad sampling of 38M
- Non-redundant: 95% deduplication removes similar sequences
- Practical: Completed in <20 minutes vs weeks for full deduplication
- Target size: 246,458 sequences fits your 200k-300k requirement
